## A problem I would genuinely like to solve

One problem I have noticed is that students often have access to a lot of resources but still don't know what exactly to learn or in what order. There are tutorials, courses and videos everywhere, but sometimes having too many options itself becomes confusing.

I would like to build something that can guide a student based on what they already know and what they are trying to achieve, instead of just giving them another huge list of resources. I think this matters because a lot of time gets wasted not because people don't want to learn, but because they don't know what to do next.
